The 3-minute "Welcome to The Table" video is structurally a Story Sunday compilation: Brett frames the opening and close, four members speak in their own voices in between. Light cleanup applied to YouTube auto-transcript artifacts (e.g. "molded over" → "mulled over"). Speaker IDs not assigned — for accuracy, names should be confirmed against the video itself before any quote is published.

## Member testimonies

### On not having to perform

> *"I felt like there was that closeness you get from family — but without having to pretend to be your best self. Just as God loves me as I am, most of the people here love me as I am."*

— Member of The Table, welcome video (~0:33)

**Possible placements:** the LGBTQ section of *What Sundays Are Like* (replaces my drafted line about "you'll just be here" if a real testimony is preferred); a Story Sunday entry; About / Our Story body.

---

### On not feeling alone

> *"We felt so alone for so long. Being a part of this community has been such a gift — meeting people who are different than us, and people who feel the same way we do."*

— Couple at The Table, welcome video (~0:55)

**Possible placements:** Our Story body; pull quote near the founding-story narrative.

---

### On the parking lot moment

> *"My first Sunday was full of anxiety. We pulled up in the parking lot and I wanted to go back home. Over the last year I've been so thankful that I did not — the healing that has been needing to take place in me required that risk. It was a risk worth taking."*

— Member of The Table, welcome video (~1:50)

**Possible placements:** *What Sundays Are Like* — the *"If you need to leave"* or the parking section, as a callout that says "this person almost did, and didn't." This is the literal Quinn moment narrated by someone who lived it.

---

### On waiting before showing up

> *"I mulled it over for two or three weeks before I actually ended up coming. And it's really not as scary. You could be like me and just sit in the back for the first three weeks. (But I might find you and just say hi.) I've been here three years now, and I wouldn't change it for anything."*

— Member of The Table, welcome video (~2:18)

**Possible placements:** *What Sundays Are Like* — the "People" section, where I drafted *"You can come in, sit in the back, and leave."* This testimony validates and grounds that line in real experience. Also strong as a Story Sunday entry.

## Brett's framing

### Opening — what The Table is

> *"Welcome to The Table — a place where visionaries are nurtured through the love and teachings of Jesus. Our mission is clear: to create a community where everyone, especially our LGBTQIA+ siblings, can thrive in faith."*

— Brett Tilford, welcome video (~0:00)

**Possible placements:** Our Story body opener; About page lede.

---

### Mid-video — what we believe

> *"We believe in a church where inclusivity, justice, and love are not an afterthought but a foundation. A place where the church's past judgments and stigmas do not define your role or your ability to participate and to lead. At The Table we celebrate every story. We've witnessed many find a home here — a sanctuary where being yourself is not just accepted but cherished."*

— Brett Tilford, welcome video (~1:20)

**Possible placements:** This is the strongest single block of Brett's voice in the video. The line *"the church's past judgments and stigmas do not define your role or your ability to participate and to lead"* lands harder than anything currently in the existing Our Story markdown. Strong candidate for a callout block on the About / Our Story page or as the bones of a values explanation.

### Closing — the seat

> *"We're thrilled to have you experience the joy, the love, and a profound sense of belonging. We're saving you a seat at the table."*

— Brett Tilford, welcome video (~2:42)

**Possible placements:** Plan Your Visit — could close that page; or as the final line of Our Story.
