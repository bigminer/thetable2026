MeetUps are diverse small groups that extend relationships beyond our Sunday services. They include home groups, book clubs, discipleship groups, and Bible studies. These gatherings offer meaningful connections centered around Christ. Our goal is for these groups to be accessible to everyone, especially new visitors, and to reflect our values of thoughtful, inclusive, eclectic, communal, and vulnerable faith.

## Current Available MeetUps at The Table

Intergenerational groups that meet for relational connection and Christ-centered discussion. Most groups share their highs, lows, and prayer requests and discuss the week's sermon topic.

### Affinity Groups

These groups vary in focus. Check out their descriptions below to learn more.

#### Book Club

- 8:00 am Sunday mornings in Wylie
- Our MeetUp spends 50% of the time eating and connecting relationally and 50% discussing a chapter of whatever book we're reading. We generally lean toward progressive Christian theology, but nothing too academic. All are welcome.
- Leader: Brett Tilford

#### Table Teens

- 6:30pm to 8:00pm Sundays
- A group for 7th-12th graders in a relaxed atmosphere that includes food, games, song, and Bible discussion.
- Leaders: Maggie Tilford, Michael Alvarez, and Marisa Martinez

#### 20-Somethings' Monthly Brunch

- Once a month, typically 11am on Sunday in Allen
- Anyone post-high school but not yet 30 is welcome for food, games, and fun conversation.
- Leaders: Christian and Megan Gray Hering

### Sprint Groups

These groups meet for a short period of time, like a month. Topics vary by group.

#### Not Your Mama's Sunday School

- Sundays at 3pm, running for several weeks before taking a break and returning
- For all who want to read and discuss the Christian Bible in a way that considers scholarship and welcomes diversity of thought.
- Leader: Megan Gray Hering

## How Can I Pitch In?

We are open to starting new groups.

Leading: As you can see from the list above, our groups are quite diverse. Your first step is to think about what kind of group you are interested in facilitating. Does it have a particular focus or meet a particular need, or is it more of a home group? Is it ongoing, or does it meet for a short season? Next, let us know.

Hosting: We are extremely thankful for those who open their homes for a MeetUp to meet. If you have a space and would be willing to participate in a group, let us know.

Interested in facilitating or hosting a MeetUp? Tell us your ideas [here](https://thetabletx.churchcenter.com/people/forms/84828).
