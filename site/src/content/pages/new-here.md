## Plan Your Visit

Plan to arrive a few minutes early to find a seat and expect to be greeted by smiling, friendly faces. All are welcome here at The Table and we hope you feel comfortable joining us.

## What's Next? The Table Class

If you have been coming to The Table for a few weeks and have begun to ask the questions: "What's next?", "Where can I find out more?", or "How do I join this community?".

The answer to all of those questions can be found at our **_Welcome to the Table Class_**.

These classes are typically held on Sunday afternoons from 3:00pm-4:30pm and childcare will be available. Feel free to [send us a message](/contact-us/) to find out when the next class will be offered.

## Want Someone to Contact You?

If you would like someone to contact you, [please send us a message](/contact-us/) and we will be in touch.
