Brett argues that real change does not come from chasing quick relief through habits that numb fear, loneliness, or pain. Preaching from Ephesians, he calls people to a deeper turning: facing what they have been avoiding through prayer, silence, confession, and a renewed capacity to love rather than use others. The message fits the *Cultivating Calm* series by treating inner peace as a practiced reordering of desire, attention, and response rather than a momentary emotional fix.
