Brett reframes fasting and other spiritual practices as training in how to stay present to discomfort instead of escaping it. Rather than numbing fear, anxiety, or shame, he describes the "emotional shake" as the moment we can learn a new way of being: recognize the urge, refrain from reacting, relax into the feeling, and resolve to keep going. The message fits the *Cultivating Calm* series by treating inner steadiness as something formed through practice, honesty, and patient love rather than quick relief.
