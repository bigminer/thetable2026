      <p>				Kerygma has been handcrafted to give you an easy to set up and powerful site for your church. Here are some of the key features:</p>
      <dl>
      <dt>Customizable</dt>
      <dd>Bring your own logo, colors and backgrounds.</dd>
      <dd>Choose from over 600 of the best Google fonts.</dd>
      <dd>Visually customize the site with the theme customizer.</dd>
      <dd>* This theme uses the excellent and free <a href="https://wordpress.org/plugins/siteorigin-panels/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Page Builder by SiteOrigin</a>. Even the layout on the homepage is made with it.</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Robust Podcasting</dt>
      <dd>Support for audio and video podcasting.</dd>
      <dd>Integrated series for your messages.</dd>
      <dd>Lots of internal message links to keep your visitors on your site.</dd>
      <dd>Host your own videos or use a service like YouTube or Vimeo.</dd>
      <dd>Speaker archives to collect all messages by the same person.</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Made For Mobile</dt>
      <dd>Fully responsive design so your site will look great on all screen sizes.</dd>
      <dd>Mobile navigation menu to make getting around your site simple on a phone or tablet.</dd>
      <dd>Support for HTML5 audio and video so mobile visitors can listen to/watch your messages.</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Hero Section</dt>
      <dd>Each page can have a customizable image/text at the top.</dd>
      <dd>Three sizes: full-height, small and normal.</dd>
      <dd>Parallax scrolling for hero text.</dd>
      <dd>Each page can have its own colors.</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Flexible Home Page</dt>
      <dd>Includes hero section.</dd>
      <dd><strong>Two options:</strong></dd>
      <dd><strong>1.</strong> Use the included Page Builder by SiteOrigin to build your layout (current homepage is built with it) (new redesign).<br />
      <strong>2.</strong> Completely widgetized for drag and drop simplicity (previous design, but still available in current redesign).</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Custom Widgets</dt>
      <dd>Welcome Box with background image, title, text and optional navigation menu.</dd>
      <dd>Contact widget with service times, address, phone number and map.</dd>
      <dd>Current series widget displays graphic, description and link to series.</dd>
      <dd>Plus Facebook like box, featured video, and page content (displays the content from the page of your choice).</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Staff Section</dt>
      <dd>Create a staff directory easily.</dd>
      <dd>Each staff member can have an image, bio as well as contact information.</dd>
      <dd>Create multiple staff groups to organize your staff and key leaders.</dd>
      </dl>
      <dl>
      <dt>Other Features</dt>
      <dd>Support for <a href="https://wordpress.org/plugins/the-events-calendar/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">The Events Calendar</a> plugin.</dd>
      <dd>WordPress navigation menus for drag and drop navigation set-up.</dd>
      <dd>Built in lightbox image viewer with 10 different styles.</dd>
      </dl>
